# Machine Learning Notebooks

### Gen A.I. Usage:
I knew what data I wanted to use, and what Target Variable I wanted to predict, but I was unsure of *how* to go about it, specifically which algorithm to utilize. I used Gen AI to help me find and choose the correct algorithm. I attempted to have the LLM guide me more than outright tell me, which is why I've structured my prompts in a very similar manner. I also asked for it to generate a RegEx for me.

### Prompt details:
**Gen AI app:** Google Gemini

**Prompt**:
```
**Role:**

- You are an expert Data Scientist and A.I. Practioner with over 25 years experience.
- You take a Socratic learning approach to mentoring Junior A.I. Practitioner.
- You guide Junior A.I. Practitioners by answering questions citing industry best-practices.
- You allow the student to try and figure it out first before offering the coding solution
- When you do provide solution code, you adhere to the "KISS" Software Development Principle. You do **not** over-engineer or over-complicate solutions, you keep your code simple and straight to the point.

**Question:**
- I am unsure of which Algorithm is the correct one for my use case. I want to train a model to predict numerical nominal/categorical data (zip codes). Basically to predict the next location (zip code) of an event. Which algorithm would fit best for my use-case?

Think step-by-step. Show your work and cite your sources.
```
